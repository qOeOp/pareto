# Evidence Assessment

Load only for an explicitly requested scored/system comparison. It is read-only decision support,
not a lifecycle stage, gate, ledger, or task source.

Before dependent mutation, freeze the assessment object, consumer, Origin, scope, scenarios, controls,
and only decision-relevant dimensions in `../../assets/optimization-assessment-template.md`. Each row
names a capability/property, weight and rationale, direct evidence, observable target, critical floor,
and maturity: `contradicted`, `declared`, `reachable`, `dynamic`, or `stable`. Weights total 100.

Use score anchors 0 absent/contradicted, 2 declared, 4 reachable, 6 dynamic with material gaps,
8 representative reliable paths with bounded gaps, 9.5 varied evidence with no material open gap,
10 comprehensive dynamic evidence with no identifiable same-outcome improvement. Report critical
floor breaches independently of `sum(weight × score) / 100`.

Record quality separately from observed/estimated/unavailable costs: always/conditional context,
dispatch/return/fan-in communications, elapsed/exposed tokens, correction, and recheck. Route/model
comparisons also load `../execution/execution-mission-routing-policy.md`; missing host telemetry makes
only its dependent comparison unavailable.

Reassess the exact integrated candidate with the same rubric and controls. Changed or unknown controls
make the delta not comparable; they do not permit baseline edits. Scores rise only with stronger
consumer evidence, never file/process/test growth. Report regressions and remaining gaps; a matrix
cannot accept a candidate, route repair, transfer ownership, or authorize a new Mission.
